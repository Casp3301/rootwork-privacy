A quiet place to track pain.

Rootwork is a personal log for pain, medication, and chronic conditions — built for the days when filling in a form is the last thing you want to do.

Join Early Access See what it does

iPhone first. More platforms this fall.

Everything you enter stays on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

That is not a marketing line — it is how the app is built. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no advertising. Rootwork has nothing to sell because it never collects anything in the first place.

What it does

Track what actually happened, not what you can remember.

Chronic pain is hard to describe from memory, and appointments are short. Rootwork keeps the record so you can point at something real.

Paint where it hurts

Mark pain directly onto a 3D figure instead of picking from a list. Each area keeps its own intensity, and the map fills in over time.

PainFrame

Time an episode as it happens — how long it lasted and how it changed — rather than guessing at it afterwards.

Medication, honestly

Doses, schedules, and how consistently you actually take them — including the ones you skipped, which is often the useful part.

Patterns, not verdicts

Trends across weeks and months, weather and pressure alongside your entries. Descriptions of what you logged — never a diagnosis.

Condition trackers

Built-in tracking for POTS, EDS, fibromyalgia, migraines, arthritis and disc problems — or build your own from scratch.

Your data, portable

Export everything whenever you like, in a file you control. Clear it all in one tap. No copy exists anywhere else.
