Everything you enter stays on your device.

Not a marketing line — it is how the app is built. No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting. Rootwork has nothing to sell because it never collects anything in the first place.

What it does

Track what actually happened, not what you can remember.

Chronic pain is hard to describe from memory, and appointments are short. Rootwork keeps the record so you can point at something real.

Paint where it hurts

Mark pain straight onto a 3D figure instead of picking from a dropdown. Each area keeps its own intensity, and the map fills in as a heatmap over time.

PainFrame

Time an episode while it is happening — how long it lasted, how it changed — rather than reconstructing it hours later.

Medication, honestly

Doses, schedules, and how consistently you actually take them — including the ones you skipped, which is usually the useful part.

Patterns, not verdicts

Trends across weeks and months, with barometric pressure and weather beside your entries. Descriptions of what you logged — never a diagnosis.

Condition trackers

Built-in tracking for POTS, EDS, fibromyalgia, migraines, arthritis and disc problems — with stand tests, joint atlases, and trigger logs. Or build your own.

Your data, portable

Export everything whenever you like, in a file you control, ready to hand to a doctor. Clear it all in one tap. No copy exists anywhere else.

Sixteen gardens

An app you can stand to look at on a bad day.

Every garden is a full palette — paper, ink, and accents — with its own drifting particles. Fireflies at midnight, petals in spring, meteors after dark. Two are earned by showing up rather than chosen.
