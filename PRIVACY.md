# Privacy Policy — Rootwork

**Last updated: 3 August 2026**

Rootwork is a personal symptom log for pain, medication, and chronic conditions.
This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your
information. It is written to be specific rather than reassuring — every claim
below describes actual behaviour of the app.

## The short version

**Your health information never leaves your device.** Rootwork has no user
accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no server that
receives your entries. Nothing you record is transmitted anywhere.

## What Rootwork stores, and where

Everything you enter — pain entries, medications and dose logs, journal entries
and photos, flares, PainFrames, care events, condition logs, body-map drawings,
settings and preferences — is stored **only in local storage on your device**.

Journal photos are written to the app's private data directory on your device.
No copy is uploaded.

We cannot see any of it. There is no account to log into, and no mechanism by
which your entries reach us or anyone else.

## What leaves your device

Rootwork makes network requests in exactly two situations, both optional:

**1. Weather and air quality.** If you enable weather features, the app sends
your approximate coordinates to **Open-Meteo** (`open-meteo.com`) to retrieve
forecast, historical and air-quality data for your area. No health information
is included in these requests.

**2. Place names.** To display a readable location name instead of raw
coordinates, the app sends your approximate coordinates to **BigDataCloud**
(`bigdatacloud.net`) for reverse geocoding. No health information is included.

These requests contain coordinates only. They do not contain your entries, your
name, an account identifier, or a device identifier assigned by us. As with any
internet request, the receiving service can observe the originating IP address;
that is a property of how the internet works, not data Rootwork sends
deliberately. Their handling of it is governed by their own privacy policies.

If you never grant location access and never enable weather features, Rootwork
makes no network requests at all.

## Location

Location is used only to fetch weather and to label entries with a place name.
It is requested through the operating system, so it is only available if you
grant permission, and you can revoke it at any time in iOS Settings. Coordinates
are used at coarse precision — enough to identify local weather conditions, not
to track your movements. Revoking permission disables those features and nothing
else.

## Fonts and other assets

All fonts and images are bundled inside the app. Rootwork does not load fonts,
scripts, stylesheets, or images from third-party servers, so opening the app
does not announce itself to anyone.

## Research

Rootwork contains code for an optional, opt-in research contribution feature.
**It is disabled and has no destination server configured.** It transmits
nothing. If that ever changes, it will require your explicit, informed consent
first, it will be opt-in rather than opt-out, and this policy will be updated
before any data is sent.

## Analytics, tracking and advertising

There are none. Rootwork contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no
tracking pixels, and no third-party telemetry. Nothing about your use of the app
is measured or reported. Rootwork does not track you across apps or websites and
does not build a profile of you.

## Data sharing and selling

Your data is never sold, rented, shared, or disclosed — because it is never
collected in the first place. There is nothing on our side to sell or hand over.

## Exporting and deleting your data

You can export your data at any time from within the app; the export is written
to a file you control and shared through the standard iOS share sheet. It goes
wherever you send it and nowhere else.

You can delete everything from **Settings → Clear all data**, which permanently
removes all entries and deletes stored journal photo files from the device.
Deleting the app also removes all of its local data. Because nothing is stored
on a server, deletion on your device is complete deletion — there is no backup
of your entries held by us.

## Children

Rootwork is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information
from anyone. As no information is collected, no information about children is
collected.

## Medical disclaimer

Rootwork is a personal record-keeping tool. **It is not a medical device, and it
does not provide diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or medical advice.**
Patterns, trends and summaries it displays are descriptions of what you recorded,
not clinical findings. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about
your symptoms and treatment. Never disregard or delay professional medical advice
because of something you saw in this app.

## Beta testing

During TestFlight beta testing, Apple collects information about installations,
sessions and crashes, and testers may submit feedback and screenshots through
TestFlight. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and is separate
from Rootwork — your health entries are not part of it. Crash reports may include
technical diagnostic data about the app, but not the contents of your entries.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change with it. If a
future version ever involves transmitting personal information, that will be
described here clearly and will require your consent.
